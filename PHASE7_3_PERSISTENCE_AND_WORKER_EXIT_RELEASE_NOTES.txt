MAG CAMP 7.3 - Persistent Storage + Worker Exit Workflow

1) Persistent storage on Render
- Database moved to /var/data/mhoms.db through DATABASE_PATH.
- Uploads moved to /var/data/uploads through UPLOAD_PATH.
- render.yaml now provisions a 1 GB persistent disk.
- Render persistent disks require a paid web-service plan; the blueprint uses Starter.

2) Worker exit workflow
- Search worker by employee number.
- Exit types: final exit, left and did not return, absconding, deceased, project transfer, resignation, termination, other.
- Approval sequence: Housing Manager then Support Services Manager.
- After final approval the worker is removed from active accommodation, room/bed becomes vacant immediately, and the complete worker record is copied to a separate archive.

3) Excel export
- Exports current and exited workers in one workbook with full available details and exit status.
